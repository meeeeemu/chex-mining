Version Changelog

THE BIGGEST CHEX UPDATE TO EVER... V0.3.2

i genuinely cannot even begin to describe all the changes that has happened to this game since i last updated it
literally all i can say is that absolutely everything and i mean EVERYTHING has changed, from the way gears are handled, to the literal luck itself
(the luck was broken for a really long time lol)
here are a couple MAJOR changes:

New Gears

+ Wheel of Fate (Tier 5) - Has a 1/300 chance to pause mining and spin a wheel which contains many effects.

+ Dinner Bomb (Tier 4) - Has a 1/65 chance to mine 70 - 120 blocks on each block broken.

+ Chexium Chronograph (Tier 4) - Has a 1/200 chance to give +0.01x to +0.2x luck for 10 seconds depending on the time of day.
(+0.2x happens at 12:00PM, +0.01x happens at 12:00AM, times are local to your timezone)

+ Chexestine Nuke (Tier 5) - Has a 1/280 chance to mine 250 - 650 blocks on each block broken.

New Pickaxes

+ Urleinator Prime (Tier 6)
--- Luck: +0.125x
--- Speed: 135ms
--- Blocks Mined: 3

+ Chexite Staff (Tier 7)
--- Luck: +0.125x
--- Speed: 135ms
--- Blocks Mined: 3
--- Ability: Has a 1/100 chance on every block mined to temporarily boost a stat for a random duration of time, this boost stacks with other temporary buffs.
> Has a 1/1200 chance to activate chex-fusion-burst mode for 12 seconds, causing all stats to be heavily infused with chex. (+0.4x luck, -15ms mining speed, +2 blocks mined)

yep! you saw correctly! Ability! not only can pickaxes have abilities now, its easier than ever for me to add them!
the entire way this game is structured has been completely rewritten to allow for even more modularity than we had before!
now allowing for pickaxe abilities, gear abilities, and quite literally interaction with any aspect of the game

you may be wondering, why are gears lagging behind in terms of tier!
because i havent thought of any tier 6 gears yet :)
